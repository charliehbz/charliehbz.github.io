eBay cross-border shipping: shorter delivery promises, but can they be kept?eBay 跨境物流｜更短的预计送达时间，能否兑现？

EDD (estimated delivery date) is the delivery date a buyer sees when placing an order. eBay's model team built a new model that uses past shipping data to predict delivery dates more accurately for orders from Greater China cross-border sellers, and rolled it out in phases. This case is about monitoring after launch: how many orders the model reached, how many actually used its date, and whether the shorter promises hurt on-time delivery or buyer complaints.预计送达时间（EDD）是买家下单时看到的预计送达时间。eBay 的模型团队开发了一个新模型，用历史物流数据为大中华区跨境卖家的订单预测更准确的送达时间，并分阶段上线。这个案例讲的是上线之后怎么监控：模型覆盖了多少订单、真正采用了多少，以及更短的承诺有没有影响准时送达和买家投诉。

Context背景

The old rules promised long; the model's date is used only when it is shorter原规则的承诺偏长，模型给出的时间更短时才可能替换

The platform used to set the delivery date with fixed rules. The rules were cautious, so buyers often saw long delivery promises, which can put them off buying. A promise that is too aggressive brings the opposite problem: parcels arrive late and buyers complain. The delivery date is a trade-off between conversion and fulfilment.过去平台按规则计算预计送达时间。规则偏保守，买家常看到较长的配送承诺，可能因此犹豫下单；可承诺如果过于激进、实际送不到，又会招来投诉。预计送达时间本身就是在转化和履约之间取舍。

The model covers orders that Greater China sellers ship from their home region, on the shipping options and destinations on the model's rollout list. For each such order the system works out two dates, one from the rules and one from the model. The model's date can replace the rule's only when it is shorter.模型的使用范围是大中华区卖家从本地发出、且运输方式和目的地在上线名单内的订单。对这些订单，系统会同时算出两个时间：一个来自原规则，一个来自模型。模型给出的时间更短时，才可能替换原规则的时间。

Coverage and adoption覆盖与采用

Eligible orders do not all end up with the model's date符合条件的订单，不一定都用上了模型的时间

The first two numbers the dashboard keeps apart. They have different denominators.这是看板里最先要分开的两个比例，它们的分母不同。

Picture a route where every order meets the model's conditions. Coverage on that route is 100%, yet only some of its orders may switch to the model's date. Each order compares two dates; where the rule's promise is already short, the model has nothing shorter to offer and the order keeps the rule's date.设想一条物流线路，上面的订单全部符合模型的使用条件，这条线路的覆盖率就是 100%。但真正改用模型时间的订单可能只有一部分：每笔订单都要比较两个时间，如果原规则的承诺本来就不长，模型给不出更短的时间，这笔订单就继续用原来的时间。

Coverage覆盖率
Orders that meet the model's conditions ÷ all orders. Answers "how many orders can the model handle?"符合模型使用条件的订单 ÷ 全部订单。回答"模型能处理多少订单"。
Adoption采用率
Orders that use the model's date ÷ eligible orders. Answers "of the orders it can handle, how many actually switched?"采用模型时间的订单 ÷ 符合条件的订单。回答"能处理的订单里，有多少真的换成了模型的时间"。

Both rates are worked out by order count and by sales value. Adoption uses only eligible orders as its denominator. With all orders underneath, adoption would rise whenever coverage grew, and you could not tell whether the model was used more or simply reached more orders.两个比例都按订单数和成交额各算一遍。采用率的分母只用符合条件的订单：如果用全部订单，覆盖范围一扩大，采用率也会跟着涨，就分不清是模型被用得更多了，还是只是覆盖变大了。

So a low adoption rate does not by itself mean the model failed. The dashboard shows the two rates side by side for each shipping option, so the team can tell "not covered" apart from "covered, but nothing shorter to switch to".所以采用率低，不一定说明模型没起作用。看板按运输方式把两个比例并排展示，团队可以分清是"没覆盖到"，还是"覆盖了，但没有更短的时间可换"。

After adoption采用之后

Once promises get shorter, check whether on-time delivery and complaints held up承诺缩短以后，还要看准时送达和投诉有没有变差

A shorter promise is not success on its own. The dashboard reads in this order: promised days, then actual delivery, then complaints.缩短承诺本身不算成功。看板按这个顺序排：先看承诺天数，再看实际履约，最后看投诉。

Promised days · main metric承诺天数 · 主指标
The end-to-end business days the buyer is promised, split into seller handling, transit and full delivery. Each is total days ÷ number of orders, so every order counts once and a few extreme orders in a small group do not skew it. The split shows which stage got shorter.买家看到的端到端预计送达工作日数，拆成卖家处理、物流运输、整体送达三段。每段都用"总天数 ÷ 订单数"计算，每笔订单权重相同，小群体里的个别极端值不会把平均拉偏。拆开以后，能看出缩短发生在哪一段。
On-time delivery · guardrail实际准时送达率 · 护栏
Whether the parcel's delivery scan comes before the promised date. The acceptance scan shows whether seller handling stayed on time, and the share of orders with tracking checks that a change in the on-time rate is not just missing tracking data.包裹的签收扫描是否早于预计送达时间。另看揽收扫描是否准时，用来检查卖家处理环节；再看有物流跟踪记录的订单占比，防止准时率的变化只是因为跟踪数据变少了。
Item-not-received rate · guardrail未收到货投诉率 · 护栏
Share of orders with an item-not-received (INR) claim within 30 days. Claims take time to arrive, so each week is flagged once it has a full 30 days behind it, and recent weeks can be left out.30 天内发起"未收到货"投诉（INR，Item Not Received）的订单占比。投诉要过一段时间才会到齐，所以每周都标了是否已满 30 天观察期，读数时可以排除还没成熟的近几周。
How to read it怎么读
Fewer promised days with on-time delivery flat or higher means the model found slack the rules had left. A clear drop in on-time delivery means promises were cut too far. A flat complaint rate fits the goal; claiming fewer complaints would need a drop on comparable orders.承诺天数下降、准时送达率持平或上升，说明模型找到了原规则多留的余量；准时率明显下降，说明承诺压得过短。投诉率持平与上线目标相容；要说投诉体验改善，需要在可比订单上看到投诉下降。

The dashboard can also split orders into those that used the model's date and those that did not. The two groups are not randomly assigned, so differences between them are used to diagnose shipping performance, not read as the model's effect.看板也能按"是否采用模型时间"把订单分成两组对比。这两组不是随机分出来的，组间差异只用来诊断物流表现，不当作模型的效果。

Sales商业表现

Sales are read as a share of demand, then against the same weeks in past years成交额先换算成市场份额，再和往年同期的走势比

Sales (GMV) of Greater China sellers rise and fall with overall demand and the season. A plain before-and-after comparison can credit the model with a peak season or a market swing. So the dashboard first takes these sellers' share of total demand, indexes it to a base period before launch (base = 100), and compares its path with the same weeks in earlier years.大中华区卖家的成交额（GMV）会随整体市场需求和季节起伏。直接比较上线前后，容易把旺季或大盘变化算到模型头上。所以看板先算这些卖家占同期市场需求的份额，再以上线前的一段时间为基期换算成指数（基期 = 100），和往年同一段时间的走势比较。

This part is descriptive. It shows whether the share moved away from its usual seasonal path, but it cannot show that the model added sales. The data also holds only completed orders, so it cannot see visits that did not turn into orders because of the delivery date shown.这一部分是描述性分析：它能提示份额是否偏离了往年的常见走势，但不能证明模型带来了成交增量。数据里也只有已成交的订单，看不到因为预计送达时间而没有下单的浏览。

Looking back复盘

A filter can quietly change what a metric means一个筛选条件，可能悄悄改掉指标的意思

What I learned学到的

The dashboard has a global filter for model eligibility. Apply it first and coverage always comes out at 100%, because the denominator has already been cut down to eligible orders. So the coverage and adoption block reads its own copy of the data and ignores that filter. When I design a metric now, I write its denominator down first and check which filters could change it.看板有一个全局筛选"是否符合模型条件"。如果先筛出符合条件的订单再算覆盖率，分母已经被砍成了符合条件的订单，结果永远是 100%。所以覆盖与采用这一块单独取数，不受这个筛选影响。现在设计指标时，我会先写清分母，再检查哪些筛选会改动它。

If I did it again如果重做

  • Replace the manual SQL export with a scheduled refresh.把手动跑 SQL、导出数据文件改成定时自动刷新。
  • With the rollout schedule in hand, compare routes that went live with routes not yet live (difference-in-differences). That says more about cause than before-and-after plus past years.如果能拿到上线分组信息，用已上线和未上线的线路做对照（双重差分），比只看前后和往年同期更能估计因果。
  • Add confidence intervals for small shipping options, so random swings are not read as the model's effect.给体量小的运输方式加置信区间，避免把随机波动读成模型效果。
