eBay cross-border shipping: shorter delivery promises, but can they be kept?eBay 跨境物流｜更短的预计送达时间，能否兑现？

During my eBay internship I owned the monitoring of a delivery-date model's phased rollout: I set the metrics, prepared the data in SQL and built a dashboard the team uses on its own.在 eBay 实习时，我负责一个预计送达时间模型分阶段上线后的监控：定指标、用 SQL 准备数据、搭建团队自助看板。

Decide with me: should this route get a wider rollout?跟我判断一次：这条线路值得扩大上线吗？→

About 2 minutes · 3 steps in the dashboard below · illustrative data. Or jump to the AI rollout-brief prototype (a portfolio extension).约 2 分钟 · 在下方看板里走 3 步 · 示意数据。也可以直接看 AI 上线简报原型（作品集扩展）。

The estimated delivery date (EDD) is the delivery date a buyer sees when placing an order. For orders within the model's scope the system works out two dates, one from the old rules and one from the model; the model's date replaces the rule's only when it is shorter. Monitoring has to answer how often the model's date is really used, and whether the shorter promises are kept.预计送达时间（EDD）是买家下单时看到的预计送达时间。对模型使用范围内的订单，系统同时算出原规则和模型两个时间，模型给出的时间更短时才会替换。上线监控要回答：模型真正用上了多少，更短的承诺能不能兑现。

Coverage and adoption覆盖与采用

Count by口径

Coverage is out of all orders; adoption only out of eligible orders. With all orders underneath, adoption would rise whenever coverage grew, and you could not tell "used more" from "reached more". Low adoption is not proof the model failed: where the rule already promises short, the model has nothing shorter to offer.覆盖率的分母是全部订单，采用率的分母只是符合条件的订单。如果采用率也用全部订单作分母，覆盖一扩大它就跟着涨，分不清是"用得更多"还是"覆盖更广"。采用率低也不等于模型没起作用：原规则的承诺本来就短时，模型给不出更短的时间。

Each bar is one week of orders, split into the three groups. The top of the light blue is coverage; dark blue over dark plus light blue is adoption. Hover a week for both rates.每根柱子是一周的订单，分成三组。浅蓝的上沿就是覆盖率；深蓝占「深蓝加浅蓝」的比例是采用率。鼠标停在某一周可以看到两个比例。

Design detail: why a filter would make coverage read 100% by mistake设计细节：为什么筛选后覆盖率会错误地变成 100%

The delivered dashboard has a global filter for model eligibility. Apply it first and coverage always comes out at 100%, because the denominator has already been cut down to eligible orders. So this block reads its own copy of the data and ignores that filter. Since then I write a metric's denominator down first and check which filters could change it.交付的看板有一个全局筛选"是否符合模型条件"。如果先筛出符合条件的订单再算覆盖率，分母已经被砍成了符合条件的订单，结果永远是 100%。所以这一块单独取数，不受这个筛选影响。从那以后，我设计指标时会先写清分母，再检查哪些筛选会改动它。

Promised handling and transit time预计处理与运输时间

Main metric. The business days the buyer is promised, split into seller handling and transit. Each is total days ÷ orders, so every order counts once and the split shows which stage got shorter.主指标。买家看到的承诺工作日，拆成卖家处理和运输两段；每段都是"总天数 ÷ 订单数"，每笔订单权重相同，拆开后能看出缩短发生在哪一段。

Promised delivery days, by week每周的预计送达天数

On-time delivery实际准时送达率

Guardrail. Orders whose delivery scan came before the promised date ÷ orders with a delivery scan. The acceptance scan is checked too, for seller handling.护栏。签收扫描早于预计送达时间的订单 ÷ 有签收扫描的订单。另看揽收扫描是否准时，检查卖家处理环节。

Tracking coverage追踪覆盖率

Orders with tracking scans ÷ all orders. On-time can only be measured on these orders, so when this line drops, a change in on-time may only mean fewer orders were measured.有物流跟踪记录的订单 ÷ 全部订单。准时率只能算在有跟踪记录的订单上；这条线下降时，准时率的变化可能只是因为被测的订单变少了。

Item-not-received rate (30 days)未收到货投诉率（30 天）

Guardrail. Orders with an item-not-received (INR) claim within 30 days ÷ orders. Claims take time to arrive, so each week is flagged once it has a full 30 days behind it, and weeks that are not mature are left out.护栏。30 天内发起"未收到货"投诉（INR，Item Not Received）的订单 ÷ 订单数。投诉要过一段时间才会到齐，所以每周都标了是否已满 30 天，未满的周不计入。

How to read the four together. Fewer promised days with on-time delivery flat or higher means the model found slack the rules had left; a clear drop in on-time delivery means promises were cut too far. A flat complaint rate fits the goal; claiming fewer complaints would need a drop on comparable orders. The used and not-used groups are observed, not randomly assigned, so differences between them diagnose shipping performance and are not read as the model's effect.四张图怎么连起来读。承诺天数下降、准时送达率持平或上升，说明模型找到了原规则多留的余量；准时率明显下降，说明承诺压得过短。投诉率持平与上线目标相容；要说投诉体验改善，需要在可比订单上看到投诉下降。采用与未采用两组不是随机分出来的，组间差异只用来诊断物流表现，不当作模型的效果。

How it works: what the program does, what the model does它如何工作：程序负责什么，模型负责什么

1 · Program1 · 程序

Reads the filter, then works out for the window and the 4 weeks before it: coverage and adoption with their numerators and denominators, promised days by stage, on-time delivery, tracking coverage and complaints for each group, and every change. It checks data maturity: complaints need 30 days, so an unfinished window gets a status and a date instead of a number.读取筛选条件，算出本期和前 4 周的覆盖率、采用率（连同分子分母）、分段承诺天数，以及各组的准时送达率、追踪覆盖率、投诉率和全部变化值。同时检查数据是否成熟：投诉要满 30 天，没成熟的窗口只给状态和日期，不给数字。

2 · Structured input (excerpt, current scope)2 · 结构化输入（节选，当前范围）


        

        

          
3 · Model3 · 模型

          
Explains, never calculates: it may only quote numbers from the input. It answers three questions in a fixed JSON format; causes go under "can't tell yet" as things to verify; immature data gets a date, not a trend; the GMV share stays descriptive; every point names its chart.只解释，不计算：只能引用输入里的数字。按固定的 JSON 格式回答三个问题；原因只能写进"还回答不了"，作为待核查；未成熟的数据给日期、不给趋势；GMV 份额只做描述；每条要点注明对应的图。

Metrics设计监控指标

      
Broke "how is the rollout going?" into layers: coverage, adoption, promised days, on-time delivery, complaints and share of sales. Fixed the denominator of each one.把"模型上线得怎么样"拆成覆盖、采用、承诺天数、准时送达、投诉和成交份额几层，并为每个指标定好分母。

      
Data in SQL用 SQL 准备数据

      
Starting from transaction-level shipping data, flagged whether each order was within the model's scope and whether it used the model's date, then aggregated by week and by seller region, route, shipping option and other dimensions, from 2024 on. Also flagged incomplete weeks and weeks whose delivery and complaint data had not yet reached 7, 14 or 30 days.从交易级的物流数据出发，标出每笔订单是否在模型的使用范围内、是否采用了模型的时间，再按周和卖家地区、线路、运输方式等维度汇总，覆盖 2024 年以来的数据。同时标出数据不满一整周的周，以及签收、投诉数据还没满 7、14、30 天观察期的周。

Calculated the metrics in Python (pandas) and built a dashboard in Plotly and Streamlit that the team can filter and split on its own.用 Python（pandas）计算指标，用 Plotly 和 Streamlit 做成团队可以自己筛选、拆分的看板。

Context背景

      
The old rules promised long; the model's date is used only when it is shorter原规则的承诺偏长，模型给出的时间更短时才可能替换

    

    

      
The platform used to set the delivery date with fixed rules. The rules were cautious, so buyers often saw long delivery promises, which can put them off buying. A promise that is too aggressive brings the opposite problem: parcels arrive late and buyers complain. The delivery date is a trade-off between conversion and fulfilment.过去平台按规则计算预计送达时间。规则偏保守，买家常看到较长的配送承诺，可能因此犹豫下单；可承诺如果过于激进、实际送不到，又会招来投诉。预计送达时间本身就是在转化和履约之间取舍。

      
The model covers orders that Greater China sellers ship from their home region, on the shipping options and destinations on the model's rollout list. It was rolled out in phases rather than all at once.模型的使用范围是大中华区卖家从本地发出、且运输方式和目的地在上线名单内的订单。模型分阶段上线，而不是一次性全量上线。

Looking back复盘

      
A filter can quietly change what a metric means一个筛选条件，可能悄悄改掉指标的意思

    

    

      

        
What I learned学到的

        
A global eligibility filter would have made coverage read 100%, because it cuts the denominator before the rate is taken (the design detail next to the coverage chart). When I design a metric now, I write its denominator down first and check which filters could change it.全局筛选"是否符合模型条件"会先把分母砍掉，让覆盖率永远显示 100%（见覆盖与采用图旁的设计细节）。现在设计指标时，我会先写清分母，再检查哪些筛选会改动它。

      

      

        
If I did it again如果重做

        
    
          
  • Replace the manual SQL export with a scheduled refresh.把手动跑 SQL、导出数据文件改成定时自动刷新。
    • 
          
  • With the rollout schedule in hand, compare routes that went live with routes not yet live (difference-in-differences). That says more about cause than before-and-after plus past years.如果能拿到上线分组信息，用已上线和未上线的线路做对照（双重差分），比只看前后和往年同期更能估计因果。
    • 
          
  • Add confidence intervals for small shipping options, so random swings are not read as the model's effect.给体量小的运输方式加置信区间，避免把随机波动读成模型效果。
